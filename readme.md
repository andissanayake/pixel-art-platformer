# Island Adventure

Game design and planning for a 2D side-scrolling action platformer.

**Game Genre:** 2D Side-Scrolling Action Platformer  
**Visual Style:** Retro Pixel Art  
**Target Platform:** Mobile (Android and iOS)  
**Theme:** Sri Lankan towns, culture and everyday life  
**Working Title:** Island Adventure

The design below is the game. The Unity project in this repository is the code-first prototype that game will be built on. What is playable today, and how the scripts fit together, is in [This repository](#this-repository).

---

## 1. Game Overview

Island Adventure is a 2D action platformer where players travel across Sri Lanka, exploring different towns, fighting enemies, overcoming obstacles and completing challenges.

The game combines classic platforming gameplay with Sri Lankan environments, local details, familiar objects and cultural elements.

The goal is to create an entertaining adventure that Sri Lankan players can recognise and enjoy, while remaining accessible to an international audience.

### Core Game Features

- Two selectable playable characters: Boy and Girl.
- Retro pixel-art visual style.
- Six Sri Lankan town-based worlds.
- Multiple levels in each town.
- Cricket bat and slingshot as starting weapons.
- Different enemy types and boss encounters.
- Coins, collectibles and rewards.
- Hidden areas and bonus challenges.
- Town-specific environments and gameplay experiences.
- Progressive difficulty throughout the adventure.

## 2. Main Characters

Players can choose between two characters before starting the adventure.

### Character 1 – Boy

- Playable adventure character.
- Fun and energetic personality.
- Retro pixel-art animations.
- Uses the same weapons and abilities as the girl.

### Character 2 – Girl

- Playable adventure character.
- Brave and adventurous personality.
- Unique appearance and animations.
- Uses the same weapons and abilities as the boy.

Both characters have identical gameplay abilities and advantages. Character selection is based on player preference.

### Character Activities

Both characters can:

- Walk and run.
- Jump and climb where appropriate.
- Attack enemies.
- Use ranged weapons.
- Collect items.
- Discover hidden areas.
- Fight bosses.

## 3. Weapons and Equipment

### Starting Weapons

**Cricket Bat**

- Close-range enemy attacks.
- Break boxes and wooden obstacles.
- Deflect certain incoming objects.
- Useful during boss encounters.

**Slingshot**

- Attack enemies from a distance.
- Hit switches and distant objects.
- Break selected obstacles.
- Useful against flying enemies.

### Potential Future Equipment

- Coconut Launcher.
- Boomerang.
- Water Balloons.
- Kite Glider.
- Grappling Hook.
- Protective Shield.
- Spring Boots.

New equipment should introduce interesting gameplay opportunities rather than simply increasing attack strength.

## 4. Sri Lankan World Map

The adventure covers six major Sri Lankan locations.

Each location has its own atmosphere, landmarks, obstacles, enemies and challenges.

### World 1 – Colombo

**Theme:** Busy city adventure.

Environment:

- Pettah market.
- Busy streets.
- Tuk-tuks and buses.
- Shops and rooftops.
- Construction areas.
- City parks.

Gameplay:

- Avoid traffic.
- Fight street gangs.
- Jump between platforms.
- Navigate busy market areas.
- Complete rooftop challenges.

Boss: Colombo Gang Leader.

### World 2 – Galle

**Theme:** Coastal adventure.

Environment:

- Galle Fort.
- Lighthouse.
- Harbour.
- Fishing boats.
- Beaches and coastal streets.

Gameplay:

- Jump between harbour platforms.
- Avoid waves.
- Escape fishing nets.
- Explore coastal areas.
- Complete boat-related challenges.

Boss: Harbour Boss.

### World 3 – Kandy

**Theme:** Cultural city adventure.

Environment:

- Kandy Lake.
- City markets.
- Hill roads.
- Traditional buildings.
- Colourful festival decorations.

Gameplay:

- Navigate moving obstacles.
- Explore city streets.
- Avoid parade-related hazards.
- Fight enemies around the city.

Boss: Mechanical Parade Boss.

### World 4 – Ella

**Theme:** Mountain and railway adventure.

Environment:

- Nine Arch Bridge.
- Railway tracks.
- Mountain scenery.
- Tea-covered hills.
- Waterfalls and bridges.

Gameplay:

- Avoid moving trains.
- Jump across railway platforms.
- Escape chase sequences.
- Navigate mountain paths.

Boss: Train Chaser.

### World 5 – Nuwara Eliya

**Theme:** Tea country adventure.

Environment:

- Tea plantations.
- Tea factories.
- Mountain roads.
- Waterfalls.
- Traditional cottages.

Gameplay:

- Navigate slippery surfaces.
- Avoid falling tea baskets.
- Explore tea factory areas.
- Overcome mountain obstacles.

Boss: Tea Factory Boss.

### World 6 – Jaffna

**Theme:** Northern adventure.

Environment:

- Jaffna Fort.
- Palmyrah trees.
- Local streets.
- Lagoons.
- Northern landscapes.

Gameplay:

- Explore hidden passages.
- Cross lagoon areas.
- Solve environmental puzzles.
- Face the final enemy challenges.

Boss: The Main Gang Leader.

## 5. Level Structure

Each town contains three main levels.

| Level | Gameplay Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Level 1 | Introduce the town and its environment |
| Level 2 | Increase enemy encounters and challenges |
| Level 3 | Major challenge and boss encounter |

**Total planned levels: 18**

Each town should feel different. Players should experience new environments, obstacles, enemy combinations and challenges as they progress.

---

## 6. Enemy Types

The game will feature a combination of recurring enemies and location-specific enemies.

### Main Enemy Categories

**Street Trouble-makers**

- Basic close-range enemies.
- Follow and attack the player.

**Slingshot Bandits**

- Attack from a distance.
- Can occupy rooftops and elevated platforms.

**Chargers**

- Rush towards the player.
- Can crash into obstacles.

**Flying Enemies**

- Attack from above.
- Require timing or ranged attacks.

**Rolling Obstacles**

- Move across platforms and slopes.
- Can be avoided or sometimes deflected.

### Enemy Progression

- Colombo introduces basic enemies.
- Galle introduces coastal enemies and hazards.
- Kandy introduces stronger enemies.
- Ella introduces enemies that interact with moving trains and platforms.
- Nuwara Eliya introduces terrain-related challenges.
- Jaffna introduces advanced enemy combinations.

| Town | Boss | Main Challenge |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Colombo | Gang Leader | Close-range combat and charging attacks |
| Galle | Harbour Boss | Fishing nets and water attacks |
| Kandy | Mechanical Parade Boss | Moving obstacles and weak points |
| Ella | Train Chaser | Fast-paced railway chase |
| Nuwara Eliya | Tea Factory Boss | Machinery and falling objects |

## 8. Collectibles and Rewards

Collectibles give players additional reasons to explore levels.

### Coins

- Found throughout levels.
- Reward exploration.
- Used to purchase selected items.

### Health Items

- Restore lost health.
- Found in hidden areas and selected locations.

### Town Emblems

- One special emblem per town.
- Required to complete the town's main objective.

### Hidden Collectibles

- Hidden in difficult-to-reach locations.
- Encourage replaying completed levels.
- Unlock bonus rewards.

### Bonus Rewards

- Character outfits.
- Special animations.
- Additional lives or health upgrades.
- New visual effects.

---

## 9. Player Progression

The adventure should gradually become more challenging.

### Beginning

- Learn basic movement.
- Introduce the cricket bat.
- Introduce the slingshot.
- Fight simple enemies.

### Middle

- More challenging enemies.
- More complex platforming.
- New environmental interactions.
- Stronger boss encounters.

### Final Stages

- Multiple enemy combinations.
- More demanding challenges.
- Advanced boss fights.
- Final adventure completion.

Players should feel that they are making progress as they travel across the island.

## 11. First Playable Level – Colombo: Pettah Trouble

**Location:** Pettah, Colombo  
**Level Type:** Introduction and action level  
**Estimated Play Time:** 3–5 minutes

### Level Story

The selected character arrives in Pettah and discovers that a troublemaking gang is causing problems around the market.

The player must fight through the streets, overcome obstacles and stop the gang leader.

### Level Progression

**Area 1 – Market Streets**

- Explore the market.
- Learn movement and jumping.
- Collect coins.
- Avoid market obstacles.

**Area 2 – First Enemy Encounter**

- Meet street trouble-makers.
- Introduce cricket bat combat.
- Defeat the first group of enemies.

**Area 3 – Rooftop Challenge**

- Introduce slingshot combat.
- Attack enemies from a distance.
- Activate a switch to open the next route.

**Area 4 – Traffic Challenge**

- Avoid moving tuk-tuks.
- Jump over obstacles.
- Fight another group of enemies.

**Area 5 – Boss Encounter**

- Face the Colombo Gang Leader.
- Avoid his attacks.
- Find opportunities to counterattack.
- Defeat the boss.

**Level Completion**

- Collect the Colombo emblem.
- Complete the level.
- Unlock the next adventure location.

## 12. Weekly Game Design Planning

The weekly plan is dedicated to designing and improving the game experience.

It should focus on what players see, experience, unlock and enjoy.

| Week | Game Design Focus | Expected Outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Week 1 | Main characters and character abilities | Finalise boy and girl characters |
| Week 2 | Weapons and equipment | Finalise starting weapons and future equipment |
| Week 3 | Colombo world and level design | Define Colombo's three levels |
| Week 4 | Enemy types and behaviours | Finalise enemy variety |
| Week 5 | Colombo boss and encounters | Finalise the first boss experience |
| Week 6 | Collectibles and rewards | Define coins, health items and unlockables |
| Week 7 | Galle world and levels | Define coastal gameplay |
| Week 8 | Kandy world and levels | Define Kandy gameplay |
| Week 9 | Ella world and levels | Define mountain and railway gameplay |
| Week 10 | Nuwara Eliya world and levels | Define tea country gameplay |
| Week 11 | Jaffna world and levels | Define the final world |
| Week 12 | Overall game experience | Review progression, rewards and game balance |

### Weekly Planning Rules

Every weekly plan should:

1. Focus on game functionality and player experience.
2. Define the features and gameplay decisions for that week.
3. Consider how the feature contributes to the overall adventure.
4. Identify any missing gameplay elements.
5. Keep the scope appropriate for a small game team.

The weekly plan should NOT include:

- Programming tasks.
- C# scripts or code.
- Unity technical setup.
- Software architecture.
- Database or backend design.
- Technical implementation instructions.

## 13. Overall Game Vision

Island Adventure should become a fun, recognisable Sri Lankan adventure.

The main focus is not simply travelling through different backgrounds. Every town should offer a different experience through its enemies, obstacles, activities, boss fights and local identity.

**Core Vision:**

- Two enjoyable playable characters.
- Recognisable Sri Lankan locations.
- Fun action-platforming gameplay.
- Interesting enemies and boss encounters.
- Meaningful rewards and progression.
- A memorable journey across Sri Lanka.

**First Priority:** Make Colombo an enjoyable first adventure before expanding the experience across the island.

**Working Motto:** One Island. Six Worlds. One Big Adventure.

## This repository

Island Adventure is built with **Unity 6** and **C#**. Gameplay is created in code: scripts spawn the player, level, camera and HUD, and the `Main` scene stays empty. The Unity Editor menu is still named **Pixel Adventure**, which is also the name of the placeholder art pack.

The playable build today is one character, one level, walk / run / jump, coins, one patrolling enemy, a goal flag, a score and a game-over screen. Colombo: Pettah Trouble is the first level to build on that foundation. Weapons, the second character, town emblems and the other five worlds are still design.
